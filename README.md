## 👋 About SMMpro

**SMMpro** is a digital marketing and technology platform providing **social media marketing (SMM)**, **SEO**, **SMO**, **digital advertising**, **automation tools**, and **web-based solutions**.

We combine marketing expertise with software engineering, so businesses get both the **strategy to grow** and the **tools to scale**.

> 🎯 **Our mission:** make professional digital marketing and automation accessible, measurable, and effective for businesses of every size.

## 🧭 How We Work

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[🔎 Discover] --> B[🗺️ Plan] --> C[🛠️ Execute] --> D[📊 Measure] --> E[📈 Optimize]
    E -.-> B
```

| Step | Phase | What happens |
|:---:|-------|--------------|
| **1** | 🔎 **Discover** | We learn your business, audience, competitors, and goals |
| **2** | 🗺️ **Plan** | We build a clear strategy, roadmap, and timeline |
| **3** | 🛠️ **Execute** | We launch campaigns, content, and technical solutions |
| **4** | 📊 **Measure** | We track performance with real data and clear reports |
| **5** | 📈 **Optimize** | We refine and scale what works, and fix what doesn't |
